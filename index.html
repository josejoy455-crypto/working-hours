Work Settings

Unpaid leave simply means that day's daily salary isn't added to what you've earned — there's no extra penalty on top.

Daily salary is always Monthly Salary ÷ 30, a fixed standard — this doesn't change month to month even though calendar months have 28–31 days.

Normal hours float with your clock-in: if you clock in late, your shift end time shifts later too — anything beyond your shift length counts as overtime.

Weekly Off Days — tap to toggle (worked hours on these days count fully as overtime)
